About the experience

How to read these records

The Many Lives of Operator X is an interactive fiction and reading experience.

The story

A thousand years after the Great Fall, humanity is preparing to leave Earth aboard the ship that nearly destroyed it…

Within the ship, nearly all of humanity shares an existence beyond comprehension. Guided by AI and human operators, the few remaining people work through simulated lifetimes so they too can integrate into the ship. Only then can the ship depart.

Operator X is the last operator. She’s a natural with the system, but as the work intensifies, she begins to question everything. Her search for answers uncovers a forgotten past—and truths that could jeopardize humanity’s ascension.

Can she finish the work before it consumes her?

Follow the questions that interest you

Approach the recovered journals and documents like a detective arriving after the events. Who was Operator X before? What’s her connection to the Institute? Does she complete her mission? Choose the mysteries you want to investigate.

Not every detail is a clue. Some records offer evidence; others add story, atmosphere, or trivia. You decide what's interesting.

Exploring with an AI agent

The full experience is designed to be explored with an AI agent. You can read closely, or skim to find questions you want to ask. The agent can help you sort through the supplied records, organize evidence, and explore connections.

This reader has no AI chat built in. It gives you a taste of the story so you can decide whether a mystery makes you want to become a detective yourself, with an AI agent to assist you. You can also simply enjoy the journals here.
